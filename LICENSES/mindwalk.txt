Micah bundles mindwalk (https://github.com/cosmtrek/mindwalk) as the
"mindwalk" sidecar binary: upstream tag v0.5.0 (commit 68aeda6) built from
source with the patches in scripts/mindwalk/patches/ applied (scan cache
with stale-while-revalidate, repeatable --claude-dir, ?follow=1 UI mode —
see scripts/mindwalk/build-mindwalk.mjs and the card
docs/mindwalk-real-no-micahs-mind-2026-08-19.md). mindwalk is distributed
under the MIT license below; the original copyright notice and license text
are reproduced verbatim as required.

The report rubric in scripts/micahs-mind-report.mjs also derives from
mindwalk (upstream HEAD 77cd795, 2026-08-10). An earlier in-repo port of the
trace model, claudecode adapter, classifier and citymap under
src/modules/micahs-mind was removed in favor of the bundled binary.
